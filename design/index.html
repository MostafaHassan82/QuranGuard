III.Newspaper · High contrast

Tahrir

Arabic broadsheet. Heavy masthead rules, condensed grotesque labels, two-column flow, dropline dividers. Green used as a single accent on near-black ink.

Marakeb

Precision instrument. Phosphor green on near-black, monospace-forward, glowing cursor, command-palette command. For users who want the tool to feel surgical.
