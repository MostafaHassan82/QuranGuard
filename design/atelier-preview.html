iii. In-page panel — injected sidebar 380 px · draggable

The six verdicts & one provenance.

QuranGuard never silently alters a quotation. Severity reads right-to-left in the manuscript and high-to-low in the index: red > yellow > orange. Orange is not the flagship — wrong words are graver than a wrong reference. Light green is reserved for corrections actually applied; it is never inferred.
