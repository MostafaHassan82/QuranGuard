III. In-page panel injected sidebar 380 px

Themes — pick one, swap any time.

Both design variants read all colors through a single --q-* token set, so the extension's current "Emerald & Gold" theme can stay as the default while users opt into Manuscript Atelier (parchment + ink) or any future scheme by swapping the token block in :root. A prefs.theme field driving a data-theme attribute on <html> is enough.

Emerald & Gold — current
The shipped extension theme. Deep green primary, gold accents on cream.
